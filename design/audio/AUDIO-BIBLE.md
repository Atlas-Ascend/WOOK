# Audio Bible

## Music language
Warm lo-fi Game Boy pulse with psychedelic harmonic color.

### Motifs
- Papa Wook: loping two-bar motif that gradually becomes more confident.
- Croc found: absurdly heroic stinger.
- Raccoon: mock-threat ostinato, never genuinely sinister.
- Phone low battery: tiny descending warning motif.
- Cosmic Wook: themes from earlier acts reharmonized.

## SFX
Footsteps, menu tick, dialogue advance, item reveal, battery warning, trade confirm, quest complete, tent flap, campfire, raccoon chirp, van door.

## Implementation law
Audio must reinforce timing. Comedy stingers should never drown dialogue.
