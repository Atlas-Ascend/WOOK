# Systems Master

## Battery
Starts low. Phone actions drain it. A depleted phone changes available information rather than simply ending the game.

## Vibes
Short-to-medium-term social trust/reputation.

## Wook Karma
Long-memory consequence ledger. It is not the same thing as popularity.

## Responsibility
Character-growth stat. Earned through follow-through, care, preparation, cleanup, helping people, and solving unglamorous problems.

## Groundscore
World scavenging and serendipity economy. Some useless objects become future quest keys.

## Cash
Small, vulnerable pool. The game is funnier when $27.43 matters.

## Inventory
Intentional clutter pressure. Objects have social, quest, trade, and comedy utility.

## Phone
Contacts, messages, GPS, camera, quest log, flashlight. A powerful tool held hostage by battery.

## Choice encounters
Contextual verbs instead of violence-first grammar.

## Save / Continue
Quest state, global stats, Croc count, phone state, and consequential choices are persistent.
