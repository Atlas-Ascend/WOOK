# WOOK V3 — LEVEL TEN

WOOK is a fully authored psychedelic comedy RPG built around GB Studio.

## Player
PAPA WOOK

## Arc
Papa Wook begins as the guy asking everybody for a lighter.

Papa Wook ends as the guy who brought water, knows where camp is,
found the missing friend, charged the power bank, packed out the trash,
and can solve an actual problem.

He still needs a lighter.

## Seven Acts
1. THE CAMPGROUND — Where Are My Shoes?
2. THE FESTIVAL — Where Is Everyone?
3. THE PARKING LOT — Where Is The Car?
4. THE TOWN — Why Are We At Walmart?
5. THE ROAD — Whose Van Is This?
6. THE DESERT — Why Is Trevor Here?
7. THE COSMIC WOOK DIMENSION — Oh.
