# Papa Wook Production Bible

## Silhouette
A Papa Wook frame must remain recognizable in one-color silhouette:
- wide brim hat,
- round sunglasses,
- full beard,
- layered jacket,
- small pack / utility mass,
- sturdy lower-body stance.

## Required animation families
- idle down/up/left/right
- walk down/up/left/right
- phone use
- item acquisition
- confused
- dance
- campfire sit
- encounter negotiation
- victory
- exhaustion

## Portrait family
Neutral, confused, delighted, concerned, enlightened, annoyed, phone-focused, victory.

## Character arc in animation
Early-game Papa Wook animation is looser and slightly chaotic.
Late-game animation is more deliberate but never loses warmth or ridiculousness.
