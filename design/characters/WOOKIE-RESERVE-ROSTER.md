## 8. Character-use law

A name is not enough to justify a character. Every named NPC must own at least one of the following:

- a mechanical function,
- a quest function,
- a world-state function,
- a relationship function,
- a dungeon function,
- a memorable repeated gag,
- a meaningful callback,
- or a piece of the hero's-journey structure.

Named characters must never be created only to fill screen space.

### Example

```text
MOLDAVITE MIKE
  role: rare-item trader
  system: Groundscore / Cash / Wook Karma
  state: appears only after first venue return
  callback: item purchased in Act IV becomes useful in the Ordeal
  visual: stone pouch + frantic hands + unmistakable silhouette
```

This is a valid named character.

```text
RANDOM FUNNY NAME
  role: none
  state: none
  interaction: one disposable line
```

This remains an unnamed camper or functional NPC.
