# WOOK — WOOKIE LEXICON & CULTURE GRAMMAR

This is a tone/worldbuilding guide, not a dictionary of stereotypes. WOOK's comedy should feel affectionate, observant and specific enough that festival people recognize the culture while outsiders still understand the gameplay.

## Core vocabulary

### Wook
In WOOK fiction, a broad cultural archetype rather than a moral category. May indicate scrappy festival survivalism, improvisation, communal behavior, questionable logistics, charisma, weird object economy and unpredictable competence.

### Groundscore
An object found in the environment whose ownership is absent/unclear and which may become a resource, trade object, joke, clue or later improvised solution. In game systems, Groundscore is both a category and a point/economy concept.

### Pashmina
Clothing/comfort/social identity item. Useful for environmental storytelling, missing-item quests, character silhouette differentiation and night/cold callbacks.

### Totem
Large carried landmark/art object used by groups in crowds. Mechanically important for navigation and Totem Forest.

### GA
General Admission camping: WOOK's persistent home-base civilization. It is not a disposable tutorial zone.

### The Long Walk
The psychologically elastic distance between camp and venue. In game form it creates anticipation, hydration choices, route memory, crowd flow and audio progression.

### Side Quest
Both a literal game system and an in-world joke: people at festivals constantly generate small urgent missions. WOOK treats many of them as real mechanical loops.

### Grounded
A recurring double meaning: physically located/oriented and socially/practically capable. “Stay grounded” can be sincere, comic and mechanical without becoming preachy.

### Vibes
Short-horizon social/emotional momentum; never morality.

### Wook Karma
Reciprocal memory of how Papa treated the temporary community.

### Responsibility
The stealth Hero stat. WOOK's central joke is that Papa becomes more heroic by doing extremely ordinary useful things.

## Dialogue tone

Good WOOK dialogue is:
- short enough for cartridge text;
- character-specific;
- funny because of situation and worldview, not only random nouns;
- willing to become sincere without announcing that sincerity;
- callback-heavy;
- readable to people who have never attended a festival.

Avoid every NPC sounding like the same “crunchy hippie.” Train Station, Loki, The Wizard, Sniffany, Bufo D' Clown, Handstand Dan, Moonbeam, Dr. Bronner, Crystalpher, Amethyst and Moldavite Mike need distinct syntax, priorities and joke mechanics.

## Naming grammar

Name families can combine:
- nature/spiritual words: Moonbeam, Amethyst, Lotus, Solstice, Aura;
- object/logistics words: Train Station, Groundscore Gary, Cooler Cole, Tentstake Tate;
- altered ordinary names: Crystalpher, Sniffany;
- material/mineral language: Moldavite Mike;
- performance/music puns: Dread Zeppelin, Chakra Khan, Bongo Benji;
- road/cosmic language: Mile Marker Maya, Event Horizon Eddie, Ohm Sweet Ohm.

The name alone is never the character. The name earns longevity only through mechanical and narrative function.

## Social behaviors as mechanics

WOOK culture becomes gameplay through:
- sharing water;
- trading strange objects;
- returning lost items;
- finding people in crowds;
- remembering camp location;
- locating stages;
- lending chargers;
- keeping promises;
- navigating group plans;
- helping with camp setup/teardown;
- negotiating with absurdly confident strangers;
- accepting that some problems cannot be solved by domination.

## Comedy law

A joke should ideally do at least two jobs:
1. make the player laugh;
2. reveal character, teach a mechanic, establish world state, create a callback, or foreshadow later payoff.

The Crocs are funny and teach quest/inventory/encounter structure. The phone at 17% is funny and establishes resource pressure. The raccoon is funny and teaches noncombat conflict. The Porta-Potty Labyrinth is funny and becomes a real dungeon. Responsibility being terrifying is funny and becomes the Hero's Journey.

## Sincerity law

WOOK earns its cosmic ending only if the ordinary weekend matters first. Friendship, temporary community, music, impermanence, practical care and memory should arise from gameplay. The game never needs to lecture the player that the festival changed Papa; the changed world and changed behavior prove it.
