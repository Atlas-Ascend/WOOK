# WOOK — AUDIO / VISUAL / PRESENTATION GRAMMAR

## Visual thesis

WOOK must look authored for its target hardware, not like a concept board flattened into a background. Every qualified gameplay scene is a composition of navigable tile/background language, collision, foregrounds, actors, animation, interactables, state-dependent props, effects and UI.

## Character readability

Papa Wook must read instantly in all four directions. Principal cast members need distinct silhouette language before portrait detail is considered. Hat, hair, beard, pashmina, prop, posture, height illusion, animation cadence and held objects can all contribute to differentiation.

Tier A — Papa Wook: complete locomotion, idle, context, reaction, item, quiet, victory/exhausted and portrait families.

Tier B — principal recurring cast: four-direction or scene-appropriate movement, signature pose/animation, portrait family, state-dependent dialogue presentation.

Tier C — supporting named NPCs: distinct overworld identity, at least one signature behavior/pose when recurring, portrait only when presentation value warrants it.

Tier D — encounters/creatures: readable silhouette, encounter pose, interaction-state feedback.

## Environment readability

Every map must answer at a glance:
- where can I walk?
- where did I come from?
- what landmark should I remember?
- what is likely interactable?
- what is the visual focus?

Environmental density is desirable only when traversal remains readable.

## State variants

Day/night/4 AM/post-Ordeal/final-morning variants should change more than palette. Change actors, props, light sources, camp clutter, open/closed paths, fire state, trash/cleanup, vehicles, music sources, quest markers and interactive affordances.

## UI composition

Native resolution is the truth. Text is authored for the real box, not written as prose and left to wrap. Portrait composition must leave adequate line width. Menus use stable hierarchy and return paths. Quiet scenes remove nonessential HUD.

## Spectacle escalation

C00: small camp intimacy.
C01: broader social density and distant venue hints.
C02: first major scale reveal.
C03: full daytime festival spectacle.
C04: night lighting and crowd transformation.
C05: subtle visual rule-breaking.
C06: visual subtraction.
C07: grounded urgency rather than maximal effects.
C08–C11: mastery and increasing festival spectacle, culminating in final set.
C12: subtraction and emptiness.
C13: ordinary road.
C14: road grammar transforms into cosmic grammar.
C15: ordinary world returns with subtle residue.

## Audio thesis

Audio communicates distance, state and memory. Venue music should be locational: distant low-frequency implication before full arrangements. Camp-at-night can contain multiple sources and crossfades. Dungeons use audio clues where appropriate.

## Leitmotif families

Papa motif — initially loose/comic, later grounded without becoming solemn.

Camp motif — home-base familiarity; night and pack-down variants reveal impermanence.

Venue motif — excitement/scale; final-set version combines earlier musical material.

Loki motif — expectation disruption, rhythmic or harmonic instability.

Wizard motif — liminal clarity; sparse and recognizable.

Sniffany motif — continuity and relational memory.

Road motif — ordinary motion gradually transformed into cosmic space, then partially returns at Home.

## Silence

4 AM, Ordeal resolution, pack-down and final-home scenes require deliberate silence/low-density sound. Silence is an authored asset, not missing music.

## SFX grammar

Critical actions require distinct feedback families: item acquire, Groundscore acquire, quest update, quest complete, menu open/close, phone low battery, save checkpoint, door/switch, encounter selection, successful social resolution, wrong/blocked action and major world transition.

## Presentation qualification

A screen cannot pass because its source art looks good. Qualification occurs in the native renderer at native resolution with actual actors, text, HUD and current state. Audio transitions are judged in play, not by file existence.
