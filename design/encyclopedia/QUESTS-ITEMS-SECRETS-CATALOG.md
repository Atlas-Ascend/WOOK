# WOOK — QUESTS, ITEMS & SECRETS CATALOG

## Main quest spine

### MQ-C00 — WHERE ARE MY SHOES?
Start: Papa wakes without Crocs.
Completion: Croc Left + Croc Right acquired; completion ceremony played; venue-ready flag enabled.
Mechanical purpose: teach movement, interaction, quest state, item pickup, encounter, HUD, menu and world exit.

### MQ-C01 — GET TO THE VENUE
Prepare water/wristband/camp marker, learn route, complete Long Walk and reach security.

### MQ-C02 — THROUGH THE GATES
Resolve venue-entry state and reach first reveal.

### MQ-C03 — DAY ONE
Participate in a meaningful authored festival-day path and return with Day-One state advanced.

### MQ-C04 — GET BACK TO CAMP
Navigate exodus, return to GA and survive the opening night-side-quest transformation.

### MQ-C05 — THIS PATH WAS NOT HERE BEFORE
Identify reality drift, complete Porta-Potty Labyrinth and leave through Impossible Shortcut.

### MQ-C06 — MAKE IT BACK BY 4
Return to camp, reach quiet checkpoint and trigger dawn threshold.

### MQ-C07 — THE ORDEAL
Resolve the consequential crisis using accumulated skills/resources/relationships.

### MQ-C08 — DAY TWO
Use mastery rather than tutorial guidance; complete Totem Forest.

### MQ-C09 — FIND THE SECRET SET / FIND YOUR PEOPLE
Use social and exploration history to reach deeper night content or a valid alternate outcome.

### MQ-C10 — ONE LAST DAY
Choose final threads and close what matters before the last night.

### MQ-C11 — THE LAST NIGHT
Reach final set and complete transformation/climax gameplay.

### MQ-C12 — PACK IT OUT
Clean/pack/return/say goodbye and leave the temporary city.

### MQ-C13 — GO HOME
Escape parking, take the road, survive road-stop weirdness and continue.

### MQ-C14 — THE ROAD IS NOT THE ROAD
Complete Memory Constellation and return toward ordinary geography.

### MQ-C15 — OH.
Reach home and complete final exchange.

## Side-quest families

### Camp practical
- FIND A LIGHTER — recurring low-stakes social chain with multiple sources and callbacks.
- COMMUNITY WATER — help refill camp water; Responsibility gain.
- LOST CUP RETURN — locate owner; Wook Karma gain.
- TENT RESCUE — repair or stabilize a neighbor's camp; practical tutorial for responsibility.
- CAMP FLAG — restore a lost landmark; later improves return navigation.
- SACRED COOLER — obtain/recover ice without turning it into a combat fetch quest.

### Social weird
- THE SPEAKER OF DESTINY — find Kyle's speaker or discover a completely different truth about who had it.
- THE MISSING PASHMINA — track social witnesses, inspect camp, optional absurd false leads.
- THE VAN THAT ISN'T OUR VAN — determine whose van everyone is occupying.
- WHO HAS MY PHONE? — answer may be “you,” but the chain should still involve meaningful state and social comedy.
- MOLDAVITE MIKE'S ABSOLUTELY NORMAL TRADE — weird-item barter with later Groundscore payoff.
- MOONBEAM'S MOONWATER — optional nighttime chain with location/time conditions.

### Skill/minigame
- HANDSTAND DAN CHALLENGE I/II/III — increasingly difficult timing/balance sequence across weekend phases.
- DRUM CIRCLE — rhythm game; results may alter Vibes/affinity but never block critical progress.
- FLOWSTATE — optional timing chain in Art Grove or venue.

### Venue utility
- LOST & FOUND — return object, identify owner through contextual clues.
- WATER RUN — assist a friend without missing every main event; teaches authored schedule tradeoffs.
- TOTEM TRIANGULATION — precursor to Totem Forest grammar.
- SECRET SET LEAD — optional multi-chapter clue chain.

## Item classes

### Core supplies
Water Bottle; Half Sandwich; Sunscreen (Empty); Festival Wristband; Broken Headlamp; Lighter; Power Bank; Camp Marker; Flashlight.

### Crocs
Croc Left; Croc Right. These are quest items, visual comedy props and recurring motif objects. They may appear symbolically in C14 but should not be mechanically re-lost without deliberate narrative purpose.

### Groundscore / strange items
Mystery Carabiner; Unidentified Cable; Bent Tent Stake; Tiny Flashlight; Single Glove; Plastic Gem; Unclaimed Pashmina Clip; Weird Battery; Tiny Spoon That Is Obviously For Camping; Broken Totem Piece; Mystery Token; Miniature Rubber Frog; Laminated Map Fragment; “Sacred” Zip Tie; Completely Normal Rock.

The rule is not that every object becomes useful. The player should learn that some strange objects create surprising options later, especially during C07 and C14.

### Quest objects
Speaker; Lost Pashmina; Camp Flag; Cooler Ice; Returned Cup; Wallet; Wristband replacement token; Lost-and-Found claim; Totem clue; Wizard token; Dungeon route key; Memory fragment.

### Symbolic late-game objects
These may be renderer/state manifestations rather than inventory objects: Crocs, Water, Phone Battery icon, Campfire Ember, Map Marker, Carabiner, Wristband, Pashmina, Totem silhouette.

## Reward grammar

Rewards can be:
- practical item;
- Groundscore;
- Cash;
- Responsibility;
- Wook Karma;
- Affinity;
- route information;
- schedule information;
- shortcut;
- vendor reputation;
- memory/callback flag;
- cosmetic/presentation change;
- later recovery option.

Not every quest should pay cash or XP.

## Secret grammar

A secret must reward observation, memory, experimentation, relationship state or weird-item use. Secrets should not be random invisible-wall checks with no clue language.

### C00 secret — Behind the Van
A strange low-value object appears useless but creates an alternate solution or callback during C07.

### C03 secret — Art Grove Back Route
Observation/environment puzzle reveals alternate route and secret-set information.

### C04 secret — Camp-at-Night State
A familiar daytime prop becomes interactable only at night.

### C05 secret — Loki's Wrong Door
Following an apparently wrong instruction can reveal optional content without being required for dungeon completion.

### C08 secret — Totem Hidden Art Path
Audio/landmark clue reveals route.

### C11 secret — Final-Set Callback
Specific relationship/memory history alters where an important character appears or what visual callback plays.

### C14 secret — Groundscore Constellation
Certain weird objects change a Memory Constellation room or create an easier/sillier alternate completion route.

## Quest-state contract

Every quest defines:
- stable quest ID;
- offer conditions;
- accepted state;
- declined state when meaningful;
- objective state(s);
- completion predicate;
- one-time reward guard;
- fail/expire behavior for optional timed content;
- save persistence;
- later callback flags;
- proof tests.

No quest is considered implemented because its dialogue exists.
