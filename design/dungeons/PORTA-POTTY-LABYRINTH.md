# Porta-Potty Labyrinth

A legitimate dungeon constructed from an illegitimate architectural premise.

## Rules
- repeating doors deliberately undermine spatial certainty
- smell meter is NOT required; restraint is occasionally funnier
- Sage Trevor appears behind a door that should not have space behind it
- one Croc-related lore artifact is hidden here
- dungeon music treats the location with complete seriousness

## Exit lesson
Ownership may be a myth. Wayfinding is not.
