# WOOK — Hero's Journey Cast Placement

This document places the canonical cast across the festival-weekend hero's journey without inventing identity mappings from retired placeholders.

## Canonical cast

- Papa Wook
- Train Station
- Loki
- The Wizard
- Bufo D' Clown
- Handstand Dan
- Sniffany

## Sequence

### Prologue — General Admission Camping

Primary:
- Papa Wook
- Sniffany
- Handstand Dan

Functions:
- Papa wakes, finds Crocs, learns controls and camp grammar.
- Sniffany anchors social continuity and the first principal dialogue relationship.
- Handstand Dan seeds physical comedy/minigame grammar without requiring the entire cast at once.

### Threshold — Venue Gates

Primary:
- Train Station
- Papa Wook
- Sniffany

Train Station becomes the human route map into the larger festival world. The name should feel absurd and completely normal at the same time.

### Lit Family Reunion — Day One

Primary:
- Bufo D' Clown
- Handstand Dan
- Train Station
- Sniffany

Functions:
- Bufo connects gameplay to performance/spectacle.
- Handstand Dan escalates skill/performance side quests.
- Train Station supports movement between stage areas.
- Sniffany maintains relational continuity.

### After the Show — GA Camping at Night

Primary:
- Loki
- Sniffany
- Handstand Dan
- Papa Wook

Loki becomes the principal branching catalyst for night side quests and the beginning of altered-reality logic.

### Alternate Reality

Primary:
- Loki
- The Wizard
- Papa Wook

Loki bends the route. The Wizard helps the player recognize that the route is bending. Neither should simply explain the experience.

### 4:00 AM

Primary:
- Papa Wook
- Sniffany
- optional The Wizard callback

The comedy quiets. Social continuity matters more than exposition.

### The Ordeal

Primary:
- Papa Wook
- Sniffany
- Train Station
- The Wizard
- consequences from Loki/Bufo/Handstand Dan side quests

Everything previously treated as comedy or optional utility should become potentially useful.

### Full Festival Weekend

All canonical characters may recur in changed states.

The same geography should reveal new dialogue, new side quests, changed relationships and mastery callbacks.

### Final Night

The cast converges through performance, side-quest resolution and relationship callbacks rather than through a mandatory group photo scene.

### Road Home / Cosmic Journey

Primary:
- Papa Wook
- Train Station callback
- The Wizard callback
- Loki callback
- Sniffany memory/continuity

Reality, memory, road, festival and cosmos progressively overlap.

### Final beat

`OH.`

## Law

Character placement must follow authored narrative function. Do not put every canonical named character in the first campground simply because the assets exist.
