QUEST 001

WHERE ARE MY SHOES?

START:

SHOES = 0

OBJECTIVE A:

Find Left Croc.

OBJECTIVE B:

Recover Right Croc from Moonbeam Jessica's altar.

WHEN:

LEFT_CROC_FOUND = 1
RIGHT_CROC_FOUND = 1

THEN:

HAS_BOTH_SHOES = 1

PLAYER_SHOES = 2

RESPONSIBILITY += 5

VIBES += 5

CAMP_EXIT_UNLOCKED = 1

MESSAGE:

BOTH SHOES EQUIPPED

+5 RESPONSIBILITY

NEW ABILITY:
BASIC PREPAREDNESS
